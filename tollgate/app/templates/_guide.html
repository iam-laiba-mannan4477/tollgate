What is this app?

Tollgate is a payment firewall for AI agents. An AI agent can only propose a payment; the Gate then allows it, holds it for a person, or blocks it. A person approves held payments with a one-time code. Everything here is fake money in a sandbox.

Words used in this app
Gate
The rule checker that looks at every proposed payment and allows it, holds it for a person, or blocks it.
Held
A payment the Gate will not send until a person approves it.
Approve with code
Approving a held payment with your signed-in session plus a fresh one-time code.
Stamp
A single-use permission, made when you approve, that works for 5 minutes for exactly one amount and one recipient.
Cooling-off
A 15-minute wait after approving a big payment, so it can still be cancelled.
Circuit breaker
A switch that stops all sending when something looks wrong; only a person can reset it.
Sentinel
The watchman that checks settings, the log and files on a schedule, and may only tighten things.
Audit log
The record of every decision, chained so that edits are detected.
Untrusted text
Message text from customers or tools, shown as data and never followed as instructions.
Throwaway world
A temporary practice copy used by the drills and deleted afterwards, so nothing live is touched.
Demo mode
A practice mode with fake money, a documented fake account and a public fake secret.
Real mode
The owner's mode, where codes come only from your authenticator app.
